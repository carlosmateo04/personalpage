The cut each platform gets

A clip is made once, at the length set under Clips. Where a platform's rule asks for a shorter one, Clipper makes a second version around the moment the clip builds to, with its own captions. A clip shorter than a platform's minimum is not sent there. 0 means no limit. These are the platforms' format rules for paid programmes; being paid also depends on the platform accepting the videos as original, which no cut or caption settles.

Your own gameplay

Folders your own recordings land in (Xbox Game Bar, NVIDIA, OBS). Every run takes new recordings from them first, as your own footage: no credit line, and nothing a platform can call a repost. A file still being recorded is left for the next run.

Rules for being paid

Beyond length, each platform has rules a video must meet before it earns anything. These are built into every clip as it is cut, and each clip shows a “Monetization check” per platform. One rule no edit can meet: all four pay only for original videos, meaning your own footage or your own commentary. A clip of another channel's gameplay says so in its check.

Alerts on your phone

A message when clips are ready, a post fails, a sign-in is running out or a claim comes in. Sent through ntfy: install the ntfy app on your phone, subscribe to a topic with a long name nobody could guess, and save the same name here. Messages say what happened and nothing else.

Clipper on your phone

Lets a phone on your home Wi-Fi open this app with a private link, to look at clips and post them. Off, nothing but this computer can reach the app. The link works on your home network only and is not encrypted, so use it at home, not on shared Wi-Fi.

More to switch on

Comments and earnings need further permissions: after switching them on, connect YouTube (and Facebook/Instagram for comments) again under Accounts.

Channels

A second game or a second brand gets a channel of its own: its own accounts, searches, schedule, clips and money. The daily run and the poster go through all of them.

Safety

The records (ledger, settings, money, rights) are backed up once a day; the newest 14 are kept. Keys and sign-ins are not in a backup: they stay in this computer's credential store.

Claude, the built-in editor

Claude is shown each video as a storyboard and picks the moments worth clipping. It then checks every clip, moves the cut to where the action starts, and writes the title, the headline, the search tags and short on-screen notes. Without it, Clipper uses its loudness finder and rule-based titles.
